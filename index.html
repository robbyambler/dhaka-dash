Dhaka Dash · ঢাকা দৌড়

Dhaka Dash is a free arcade game about crossing the road in Dhaka. You guide Rupa, a student with a backpack and an orange scarf, across ten busy streets while rickshaws, buses, CNG auto-rickshaws, motorbikes and trucks rush past. It plays in English and in Bengali.

The ten places are Dhanmondi Lake, New Market and Nilkhet, Shahbagh, Farmgate, Karwan Bazar, Hatirjheel, Mohakhali, Gulshan-2 Circle, Motijheel, and Sadarghat in Old Dhaka.

To play, use the arrow keys or WASD to hop one step at a time, watch the traffic, and wait for your gap. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons. Pick up tea and snacks along the way to sharpen your eyes and quicken your feet. You can switch between English and Bengali in the game.
